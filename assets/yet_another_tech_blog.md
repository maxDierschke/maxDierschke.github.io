# Why the world needs another tech blog 

A plethora of tech blogs exist nowadays, which range from niche topics to very general articles. Many of them are helpful resources for learning about current trends in the industry.</br>
Oftentimes, they are not only useful to get some exposure to the latest and greatest topics, but may also aid as an additional resource for technology with complicated documentation. Especially when learning about some technical topic in your free time, reading a colloquially written blog post is more pleasant than reading lengthy documentation.

After all, tech blogs don't only feed our curiosity about new ideas, but also give additional information on complex topics, which is oftentimes easier to consume than the technical documentation that is provided by frameworks, languages or other technologies.

As you are reading a blog post yourself right now, you should be aware of the value a good blog post can provide. But after this very vague introduction, you may ask yourself:

_As so many blog(posts)s already exist, does the world need yet another tech blog?_

**To be honest, it doesn't!**

<br/>

# Why I start this tech blog anyways
Despite the sheer amount of different tech blogs, I believe that there will still be a place for mine.
On one hand, I want to share my passion for software engineering, and on the other hand, I want to improve my overall capabilities as software engineer. This includes technical proficiency as well as improving my (technical) writing. 
I want to take the reader along to dive deep into technical topics and to share the learnings I have along the way.

The main topic for this blog will be to showcase the things I am working on in my free time. In the past I have been working on multiple weekend projects, where I implemented interesting algorithms from scratch or tried out different technologies (e.g. [langchain](https://www.langchain.com/)).
Sadly, though, I did not document my learnings and did not open source my code. Looking back at some of these projects, it is not easy to track back to find out what I learned from each project. Therefore, I want to report on my future projects with blog posts in order to document my learnings for my future self and anyone else that is interested.
With this practice, I aim to become a better software engineer over all.

I hope I piqued your curiosity, and you will enjoy the blog posts that are yet to come.

# Limitations of this Blog
